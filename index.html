What this is

See what's actually
happening. Before or after.

Not advice. Not a chatbot. You write what's happening — it reads what's actually there. Two reasons people use it:

— Something feels off and you want to see it clearly before it gets worse. The distance is growing. The same argument keeps happening. Nothing is broken yet but something is drifting.
— Something is already wrong and you're too close to see it clearly. You know something is there. You just can't map it.

Who it's for

Any relationship that matters
and is hard to see clearly.

—A partner you love but keep having the same argument with
—A parent or sibling where the distance keeps quietly growing
—A friendship that's been off for months and you can't name why
—A colleague or situation at work where something is wrong but you can't see the pattern

How it works

Write it. Come back.
Watch what the map shows you.

Give it a baseline — where things stand right now. Come back when something happens. A conversation, a silence, a decision that keeps not getting made. The map reads across everything you give it.
