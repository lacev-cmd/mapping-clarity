How it works

It reads what you give it.
Nothing more.

Mapping Clarity is a pattern-reading engine. You write what's happening. It counts what appears independently, tracks what is moving across time, holds contradictions, and surfaces what's missing. It doesn't generate, predict, or advise. Evidence only.

The process

Write once. Come back.
Let it read across time.

1
Write a baseline
Where things actually stand. What's been happening. Be specific — what was said, what wasn't, what keeps repeating. The more honest, the more the map finds.
2
Come back when something happens
A conversation. A silence. A decision that keeps not getting made. Add it as a new entry. The map reads across everything — not just the latest thing.
3
Read the map
What's moving and what isn't. What you keep circling without naming. What you say about the relationship versus what you describe doing in it. Gaps the map found that you didn't put in.
4
Export your report
When you're ready, export a full pattern report — for yourself, or to share with a therapist, counsellor, or someone supporting you. $9/month, cancel anytime.

What it reads

The gap between what you say
and what you describe doing.

Most people write clearly about how they feel about a relationship. What the map looks for is what you actually do — or don't do — within it.

—Movement vs stagnation — what has actually changed versus what you're telling yourself has changed
—Repair intended vs repair attempted — saying you want to fix something is not the same as doing anything toward it
—Contact described vs contact happening — closeness without contact is a significant gap
—Avoidance — what keeps not getting said, not getting done, not getting started
—Contradictions — where what you say about the relationship and what you describe in it don't match
"We've been together three years. The last few months something has shifted. We don't argue — we just don't really talk anymore. I keep meaning to say something but I don't know how to start."
